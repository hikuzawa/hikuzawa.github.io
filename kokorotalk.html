絵カードでおしゃべりサポート Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the こころトーク - 絵カードでおしゃべりサポート (Kokoro Talk) app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Aito Takazawa (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") with an optional one-time in-app purchase. The Application contains no advertising of any kind. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device.

The Application does not require account registration and does not collect your name, address, phone number, or email address.

The Service Provider may use the information you provided to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information, required notices and marketing promotions.

Content You Create in the Application

Communication cards, photos, and card usage statistics that you create in the Application are stored only on your device. They are never transmitted to the Service Provider's servers. Uninstalling the Application deletes this data from your device.

The Application requests access to your camera and photo library solely so that you can create your own communication cards. Selected images are copied into the Application's private storage area on your device and are never uploaded.

Third Party Access

Only aggregated, anonymized data is periodically transmitted to external services to aid the Service Provider in improving the Application and their service. The Service Provider may share your information with third parties in the ways that are described in this privacy statement.

The Application does not contain any advertising SDK and does not collect or share an advertising identifier.

Please note that the Application utilizes the following third-party service, which has its own Privacy Policy about handling data:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase

In-app purchases are processed solely through Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play). No third-party purchase management service is used. Payment information is handled entirely by Apple and Google, and the Service Provider never receives or stores your payment details.

Disclosure

The Service Provider may disclose User Provided and Automatically Collected Information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
  • when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
  • with their trusted services providers who work on their behalf, do not have an independent use of the information we disclose to them, and have agreed to adhere to the rules set forth in this privacy statement.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network.

Kokoro Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks additional features and does not change what data is collected.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider will retain User Provided data for as long as you use the Application and for a reasonable time thereafter. If you'd like them to delete User Provided Data that you have provided via the Application, please contact them at aito.hikuzawa@gmail.com and they will respond in a reasonable time.

Children

The Application is designed to be operated by a parent, guardian, or support professional together with a child who needs communication support. Settings, purchases, and usage reports are placed behind a parental gate so that a child cannot reach them unaided.

The Service Provider does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13. The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. In the case the Service Provider discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, the Service Provider will immediately delete this from their servers. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact the Service Provider (aito.hikuzawa@gmail.com) so that they will be able to take the necessary actions.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. The Service Provider provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect information the Service Provider processes and maintains.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes to the Privacy Policy by updating this page with the new Privacy Policy. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.

プライバシーポリシー（日本語）

本アプリ「こころトーク」における、利用者の情報の取り扱いについて説明します。

1. 個人情報の取得について

本アプリは、氏名・住所・電話番号・メールアドレスなどの個人を直接特定する情報を取得しません。アカウント登録も不要です。

2. アプリ内で作成したデータ

作成した絵カード、写真、カードのタップ回数などのデータは、すべてお使いの端末内にのみ保存されます。開発者のサーバーへ送信されることはありません。アプリを削除すると、これらのデータも端末から削除されます。

3. 写真・カメラへのアクセス

オリジナルの絵カードを作成する目的でのみ、カメラおよび写真ライブラリへのアクセスを求めます。選択された画像は端末内のアプリ専用領域にコピーされ、外部へ送信されることはありません。許可しない場合でも、絵文字カードや文字カードの作成、およびその他すべての機能をご利用いただけます。

4. 広告について

本アプリは、お子様が使うことを前提としているため、誤タップによる意図しない遷移を防ぐ目的で、アプリ内に広告を一切表示しません。広告配信のためのSDKも組み込んでおらず、広告IDの取得・共有も行いません。

5. 第三者サービスの利用

本アプリが利用する第三者サービスは、以下のみです。

  • Google Firebase Analytics（利用状況の分析）— どの画面が使われているか等の統計情報を、個人を特定しない形で取得します。

アプリ内課金は Apple（App Store）および Google（Google Play）の仕組みのみを利用しています。決済情報はApple / Googleが管理し、開発者が受け取ることはありません。課金管理のための外部サービスは利用していません。

6. お子様の利用について

本アプリは、意思伝達に支援が必要なお子様とその保護者・支援者の方の利用を想定しています。設定・課金・分析機能には保護者確認（計算問題）を設けており、お子様が誤って操作できないようにしています。

7. お問い合わせ

aito.hikuzawa@gmail.com

8. 本ポリシーの変更

本ポリシーの内容は、必要に応じて変更されることがあります。重要な変更を行う場合は、アプリ内またはストアの説明文にてお知らせします。
