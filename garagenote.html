GarageNote 車・バイクの整備手帳

車検・整備・給油記録の管理アプリ Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the GarageNote 車・バイクの整備手帳 app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Aito Takazawa (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"). This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server. There is no user account, no sign-in, and no backend service.

All data you enter into the Application — vehicles, fuel records, maintenance records, expenses, photos, and settings — is stored locally on your device only. The Service Provider has no access to this data, and it is never sent anywhere unless you explicitly export and share it yourself (for example, via the backup/export feature).

The Application does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies of any kind at this time.

Third Party Access

The Application itself does not share data with third parties. However, the Application contains links that open external, third-party services in your device's browser:

  • Amazon.co.jp — when you tap "パーツを探す" (Find parts) to search for a replacement part. Amazon's own privacy policy applies once you leave the Application.

The Application also uses your device's built-in App Store review prompt and, where supported, its native photo library / camera picker to let you attach a photo to a vehicle. Neither of these send any data to the Service Provider.

Disclosure

Because the Application does not collect or hold any personal information on the Service Provider's side, there is no personal data for the Service Provider to disclose to any third party, including in response to legal process.

Opt-Out Rights

Since no data is collected, there is nothing to opt out of. You can remove all data created by the Application at any time by deleting it within the app, or by uninstalling the Application, which removes all locally stored data from your device.

Data Retention Policy

All data stays on your device for as long as you keep the Application installed. If you export a backup file yourself, that file is your responsibility to store and delete.

Children

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children, since it does not collect information from anyone.

Security

Because your data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security (passcode, Face ID/Touch ID, disk encryption, etc.).

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, for example if advertising or cloud sync features are added in a future version. The Service Provider will update this page with the new Privacy Policy whenever that happens. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes.
